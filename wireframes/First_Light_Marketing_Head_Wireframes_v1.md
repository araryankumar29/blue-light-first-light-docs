# First Light · Marketing Head wireframes v1

Plain-text version of the wireframe set. Each board lists its phone screens with numbered components, and each requirement page lists the rules for those screens. Bracketed values such as [14] are placeholders.

_Version 1 · 22 November 2026 · for cross-check_

## First Light · Marketing Head wireframes

Four boards and four requirement pages. The Marketing Head sets the brand template, supervises the content pipeline and the posts, reads the funnel per post and channel with no personal lead details, raises spend requests the MD approves, and receives finished project packages with no client details.

Sources: sitemap pages for the Marketing Head, the access matrix and the specification's Marketing Head powers (section 15 and marketing decisions).

## Conflicts settled

| Item | What the sources say | Decision and why |
|---|---|---|
| Client details | Spec v7 and sitemap: Marketing Head sees only final files and brief, no client details. | No client details on packages. Funnel shows counts only, no lead names or contacts. |
| AI model | Spec and sitemap: platform chooses the model; Marketing Head does not choose it. | Not shown as a choice anywhere in this set. |
| Spend approval | Spec: MD approves marketing spend; Marketing Head sees full status. | As written. |
| Tab bar | Sitemap marketing screens: Home, Brand, Posts, Funnel, Spend. | Home, Content, Funnel, Spend, More. Brand sits under Home and the template is reached from the content screen. |

## Cross-check · what was verified

- No client details appear on packages or the funnel.

- The platform chooses the AI model; no screen lets the Marketing Head choose it.

- Every spend request shows status at each step, and the MD approves it.

- Brand changes are kept as versions and can be restored.

- No rupee figures are invented; placeholders only.

## Marketing Head wireframes · 1 of 4 · Home, brand, content

The Marketing Head sets the brand template, chooses content types and supervises the pipeline. The platform generates the content and chooses the AI model.

### Home

This week, pipeline counts, add own content.

**Components**

1. **Header**: Title centre. Logout top right.
2. **This week**: Posts scheduled and leads passed to the Sales Head.
3. **Pipeline counts**: Content stages from idea to published.
4. **Add content**: Add posts alongside what the system generates.
5. **Tab bar**: Home, Content, Funnel, Spend, More.

### Brand template

Logo, colours, fonts; versions kept.

**Components**

1. **Header**: Back.
2. **Logo and colours**: Logo, colours and fonts for the studio.
3. **Fonts**: Fonts for headings and text.
4. **Save and history**: Every change is kept as a version and can be restored.
5. **AI model**: The Marketing Head does not choose the model.

### Content

Items and stages; hand leads on.

**Components**

1. **Header**: Search right.
2. **Content items**: Each item with its stage.
3. **Move stage**: Move an item from idea to published.
4. **Hand leads**: Leads filtered and passed to the Sales Head, who groups them.
5. **Tab bar**: Content active.

### Post

Channels, schedule, publish.

**Components**

1. **Header**: Back.
2. **Post**: Generated by the system from the template.
3. **Channels**: Pick the channels.
4. **Schedule and edit**: Schedule a date and edit copy and media.
5. **Publish**: Publish now or queue.

## Requirements · home, brand, content

The Marketing Head sets up the studio's brand and supervises the pipeline. The platform chooses the AI model.

### Home

The home shows posts scheduled this week, the leads passed on, the pipeline counts, and a way to add the Marketing Head's own content.

**Rules and states**

- Posts and leads this week
- Pipeline counts
- Add own content

**Sources**

Sitemap home-mh; spec §15.

### Brand template

The brand template holds the logo, colours and fonts. It can be edited at any time, and every change is saved as a version that can be restored. Brand data belongs to the company.

**Rules and states**

- Logo, colours, fonts
- Every change a version
- Restore earlier version

**Sources**

Sitemap brand-template; spec on the marketing pipeline.

### Content

The content pipeline runs from idea to published. The Marketing Head adds his own content and moves items on. Leads from the channels are filtered and passed to the Sales Head.

**Rules and states**

- Stages to published
- Own content
- Leads to Sales Head

**Sources**

Sitemap content-pipeline; spec on the marketing pipeline.

### Post

The system generates posts from the brand template and schedules them across the chosen channels. The Marketing Head chooses channels and times, edits copy and media, and publishes or queues.

**Rules and states**

- Generated from template
- Channels and times chosen
- Edit before publish

**Sources**

Sitemap posts.

## Requirements · comments, funnel, channels, spend

The funnel counts each step of a post or channel. It shows no personal lead details.

### Comments

The platform answers comments and replies automatically. Questions that need a person are routed to the Sales Head.

**Rules and states**

- Automatic replies
- Questions routed

**Sources**

Spec §15 and the marketing pipeline; sitemap posts.

### Funnel

The funnel shows leads, meetings and converted clients for each post, with the percentage at each step. The Marketing Head can open a step, but sees counts only, not the leads' names or contacts.

**Rules and states**

- Per post
- Percentages each step
- Counts only, no personal details

**Sources**

Sitemap funnel; decision recorded below.

### Channel funnel

The same funnel is shown per channel, with a date range the Marketing Head can change.

**Rules and states**

- Per channel
- Date range

**Sources**

Sitemap funnel.

### Spend

Spend requests carry amount, channel and purpose. The MD approves them. The Marketing Head sees the status at every step: raised, approved, declined, paid.

**Rules and states**

- Amount, channel, purpose
- Status each step
- MD approves

**Sources**

Spec §9 (marketing spend); sitemap spend-requests.
